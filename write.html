Open for collaboration

Write for UNEVEN

UNEVEN is better with more voices. If you know a place where the costs and benefits of change are unevenly shared, we'd like to hear from you.

What you can contribute

Text

Short piece

600–1,000 words on one place and one question: who wins, who pays?

Maps

Map or GIS analysis

A map with a short explanation, built from data you can cite.

Photos

Photo story

Images from fieldwork or excursions, with captions.

Guidelines

  • Contributions are in English. Editing help is included, so don't worry about perfect language.
  • Every number needs a source: peer-reviewed research, official statistics or reliable data.
  • Only use photos and maps you made, or ones you have permission to publish.
  • You are published under your name with a short bio. UNEVEN is a non-profit student project, so there is no payment.
  • The editor has the final say on edits, and you see the final version before it goes online.

How it works

  1. Pitch. Send 3–4 sentences: the place, the question, why it's uneven.
  2. Agree. We decide together on format, length and deadline.
  3. Draft. You write or make your piece.
  4. Edit. One round of feedback and revisions.
  5. Publish. Your piece goes on the site and on Instagram.
