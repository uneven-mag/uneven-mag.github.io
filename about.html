About

Why UNEVEN?

Global change is never felt the same everywhere. This magazine maps where it lands.

Every big transition has a map

Energy, water, climate: we talk about them as global stories. But their costs and benefits land in very specific places. A lithium mine in the Atacama. A dry river in Murcia. A heat wave in a city without trees.

Look at enough maps and the same pattern appears again and again: the people who pay are rarely the people who decide. UNEVEN exists to make that pattern visible.

Why now

  • The energy transition is building new mines, supply chains and trade rules. Who profits is decided in this decade.
  • The climate crisis hits hardest where emissions were lowest, and money for adaptation is scarce.
  • Water: droughts and floods are becoming more extreme, and so are the conflicts over who gets water.

How it works

  • Original maps. Every issue includes its own GIS analysis built from open data.
  • Research, not opinion alone. Peer-reviewed studies and official statistics. Every number has a source.
  • Free to read. Every essay is published in full on this site.
